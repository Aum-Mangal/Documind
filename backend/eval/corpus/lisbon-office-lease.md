Summary of lease terms — Lisbon office, Avenida da Liberdade 212

This note summarises the main terms of the lease for Halden & Rowe's Lisbon office, signed on 3 February 2025 with the landlord, Liberdade Imobiliária S.A. It is a summary for managers and does not replace the lease itself, which is held by the legal team.

The premises are the fourth and fifth floors of the building, a total of 2,150 square metres, with 40 parking spaces in the basement and access to the shared rooftop terrace.

Term, rent and deposit

The lease runs for ten years from 1 March 2025 to 28 February 2035. Halden & Rowe may end the lease early at the end of year five, on 28 February 2030, by giving twelve months' written notice, without penalty.

Rent is 52,000 euros per month, excluding VAT, payable quarterly in advance. Rent is reviewed every year on 1 March in line with the Portuguese consumer price index, with a cap of 4 percent per year. The first six months were rent-free as an incentive.

A security deposit equal to three months' rent was paid on signing and will be returned within 60 days of the end of the lease, less any amounts owed.

Use, repairs and subletting

The premises may be used as offices and for light electronics testing, but not for manufacturing. The landlord is responsible for the structure, the roof and the building's central heating and air conditioning plant. Halden & Rowe is responsible for internal repairs and for returning the space in good condition, allowing for fair wear and tear.

Subletting up to 25 percent of the space is allowed with the landlord's consent, which may not be unreasonably refused. Any larger sublet or an assignment of the lease needs the landlord's prior written approval.

The building is insured by the landlord; Halden & Rowe must insure its own contents and hold public liability cover of at least 5 million euros.
