Halden & Rowe — Report to the Board, Third Quarter 2026

This report summarises trading, operations and governance for the quarter ending 30 September 2026. It was prepared by the finance office for the board meeting held on 14 October 2026 and should be read together with the vendor review of Northwind Supply circulated separately.

Group revenue for the quarter was 48.6 million euros, an increase of 14 percent on the same quarter last year. Gross margin improved to 41.2 percent from 38.9 percent, helped by a shift towards subscription contracts and lower freight costs in the second half of the quarter. Operating expenses grew by 9 percent, mainly because of hiring in engineering and the opening of the Porto office.

Cash at the end of the quarter stood at 22.4 million euros. Net cash generated from operations was 6.1 million euros. No new borrowing was taken on during the quarter, and the revolving credit facility of 15 million euros remains undrawn.

Regional performance

The Lisbon office was the strongest performer. New enterprise contracts signed in Lisbon added 4.2 million euros of annual recurring revenue, the largest of which was a three-year agreement with the logistics group Marisol Transportes. Lisbon now accounts for 31 percent of group revenue.

The Madrid office grew revenue by 6 percent, below its target of 10 percent. The shortfall was mostly due to two public-sector tenders being postponed into the fourth quarter. Management expects both tenders to be decided by December.

The Porto office opened on 1 August 2026 with a team of 18 people. Daniel Weiss, previously head of customer engineering in Lisbon, will lead the Porto office. Its first year is planned as an investment year, with break-even expected in the second half of 2027.

Product and operations

The launch of the Atlas X2 controller, originally planned for September, has been moved to the next quarter. The delay was caused by late delivery of revised circuit boards from Northwind Supply, the group's main component vendor. The engineering team used the extra time to complete a second round of field trials with three pilot customers, and no defects of consequence were found.

Customer support response times improved. The median first response time fell from 3 hours 40 minutes to 1 hour 55 minutes after the support team adopted a follow-the-sun rota between Lisbon and Madrid.

Staff turnover for the trailing twelve months was 11 percent, down from 14 percent a year earlier. The employee survey run in July had a response rate of 82 percent.

Governance and appointments

The board appointed Maria Okafor as chief operating officer, effective 1 November 2026. Ms Okafor joined Halden & Rowe in 2021 as director of operations and has led the integration of the support and logistics teams. She succeeds Pieter Lindqvist, who will retire at the end of the year after nine years with the company and remain as an adviser until March 2027.

The audit committee approved the appointment of Brandt & Moreira as external auditors for the 2026 financial year, replacing the previous firm after a competitive tender. The committee also reviewed the risk register and added supplier concentration as a principal risk, given the group's reliance on a single vendor for Atlas components.

The remuneration committee agreed a 3.5 percent increase in the salary budget for 2027.

Outlook

Management expects fourth-quarter revenue of between 50 and 52 million euros. The main risks to this outlook are the timing of the Atlas X2 launch, the outcome of the two Madrid tenders, and further supplier disruption.

The board will be asked at its December meeting to approve a capital expenditure budget of 3.8 million euros for 2027, most of which is for test equipment in Porto and an upgrade of the data centre in Lisbon. A proposal to qualify a second component supplier will be presented at the same meeting.

The next report to the board will cover the fourth quarter and the full year, and will be circulated in late January 2027.
