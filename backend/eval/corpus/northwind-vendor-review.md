Northwind Supply — Annual Vendor Review 2026

This review assesses Northwind Supply, the component vendor for the Atlas product line, against the service levels in the master supply agreement. Northwind has supplied Halden & Rowe since 2019 and is responsible for roughly 70 percent of component spend. The review was carried out by the procurement team between August and October 2026.

Northwind manufactures printed circuit boards and enclosures at two plants, in Gdansk, Poland, and Brno, in the Czech Republic. Until this year all Atlas boards were produced in Gdansk.

Delivery performance

In the third quarter Northwind delivered 71 percent of orders on time, down from 94 percent in the same quarter a year earlier. The contractual target is 95 percent. The shortfall came from a fire at the Gdansk plant on 9 July 2026, which damaged a reflow soldering line and halted production of the revised Atlas circuit boards for five weeks.

Quality remained good despite the disruption. The defect rate on delivered boards was 0.4 percent, within the contractual limit of 1 percent. Northwind communicated the incident within 24 hours and provided weekly recovery updates, which the procurement team rated as satisfactory.

Remediation plan

Northwind will move Atlas board production to its Brno facility by December 2026 and keep Gdansk as a secondary line once it is repaired. A dedicated account manager, Tomasz Wrona, has been assigned to Halden & Rowe for the duration of the transition.

In parallel, Halden & Rowe will qualify a second supplier, Kestrel Components, as a backup source for Atlas boards. Procurement lead Ines Varga owns the qualification and expects it to finish in February 2027. Qualification includes a factory audit, a trial batch of 500 boards and a reliability test of 1,000 hours.

Commercial terms

The current master supply agreement runs until March 2027 and renews automatically for two years unless either party gives six months' notice. A late-delivery penalty of 2 percent of order value applies to orders delivered more than ten business days late. The penalty was waived for orders affected by the Gdansk fire under the force majeure clause.

Prices are fixed until the end of the agreement, except for a pass-through of copper price changes above 10 percent. Payment terms are 60 days from invoice.

Recommendation: renew the agreement for one year only, conditional on Brno reaching 95 percent on-time delivery for two consecutive months, and complete the Kestrel qualification before the renewal date.
