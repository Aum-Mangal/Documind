Atlas X2 Industrial Controller — Product Specification, Revision C

The Atlas X2 is a programmable controller for warehouse conveyors and sorting systems. It replaces the Atlas X1, with twice the input and output capacity, a faster processor and support for redundant networking. This document describes hardware revision C, part number ZX-4471. Revision B units, part number ZX-4470, were used only in field trials and are not sold.

The controller is designed for continuous operation in industrial environments and is certified for use in the European Union and the United Kingdom.

Electrical and environmental ratings

Supply voltage: 24 V DC nominal, accepted range 18 to 32 V DC.
Typical power consumption: 14 W; maximum 22 W with all outputs active.
Operating temperature: minus 20 to plus 60 degrees Celsius.
Storage temperature: minus 40 to plus 85 degrees Celsius.
Ingress protection: IP65 when fitted with the sealed connector kit, part number ZX-4471-SK; IP40 otherwise.
Mean time between failures: 180,000 hours, calculated at 40 degrees Celsius.

The unit is protected against reverse polarity and against supply surges of up to 1 kV. An internal supercapacitor keeps the real-time clock running for 72 hours without power.

Inputs, outputs and networking

The Atlas X2 has 32 digital inputs, 24 digital outputs and 8 analogue inputs, which can be extended with up to four expansion modules over the backplane bus. Each digital output can switch up to 2 A.

Networking: two Gigabit Ethernet ports supporting a redundant ring with a failover time of under 50 milliseconds, one RS-485 port, and an optional cellular modem module. Supported protocols are Modbus TCP, OPC UA and MQTT. Remote firmware updates are signed and can be rolled back to the previous version.

Software, warranty and support

Programs are written in the IEC 61131-3 languages using the free Atlas Studio software, version 5.2 or later. The controller stores up to 64 MB of programs and logs 30 days of events locally.

Halden & Rowe provides a three-year warranty from the date of delivery, extendable to five years with a support contract. Firmware updates are provided for at least seven years after the end of sale. Units returned under warranty are repaired or replaced within ten business days.

Technical support is available from 08:00 to 20:00 CET on business days, with 24-hour emergency support for customers on the Premium support plan.
