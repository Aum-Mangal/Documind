Halden & Rowe Employee Handbook — Working Arrangements and Leave

This section of the handbook explains where and when you can work, how leave works, and what to do if you are ill. It applies to all permanent employees in Portugal and Spain. Contractors should refer to their own agreements. The policy was last updated in June 2026 and is owned by the People team.

If anything in this section conflicts with your employment contract or with local law, the contract or the law takes precedence.

Remote and hybrid work

Most roles are hybrid. Employees in hybrid roles are expected in the office on at least two days a week, chosen with their manager. Teams agree one common office day so that people who collaborate closely overlap.

You may work fully remotely for up to 20 working days a year, for example while travelling or caring for a relative. Working from another country requires approval from the People team at least four weeks in advance, because of tax and insurance rules, and is limited to 30 days in any twelve months.

Halden & Rowe provides a one-off home office allowance of 400 euros for desks, chairs and monitors, and a monthly internet allowance of 25 euros for hybrid and remote employees.

Working hours

The standard working week is 40 hours in Portugal and 39 hours in Spain. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time; outside core hours you can arrange your day with your team.

Overtime must be agreed in advance with your manager. It is compensated with time off in lieu at one and a half hours for each hour worked, to be taken within three months. Employees on the support rota receive an on-call allowance of 120 euros per week on call, in addition to time off in lieu for calls handled outside working hours.

Annual leave and holidays

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays in their country. Leave builds up during the year at just over two days per month. Up to five unused days can be carried over to the following year and must be used by 31 March; any other unused leave is lost.

Leave should be requested in the HR system at least two weeks before the first day off for absences longer than three days. Parents receive the statutory parental leave in their country, and Halden & Rowe tops up pay to 100 percent of salary for the first 16 weeks.

Each employee also has two volunteering days per year to spend with a registered charity.

Sickness absence

If you are ill, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. For absences of up to three days you do not need a medical certificate; from the fourth day a certificate from a doctor is required.

Halden & Rowe pays full salary for up to 30 days of sickness absence per year, on top of any statutory sick pay. Longer absences are reviewed case by case with the People team, who will also discuss a phased return to work where that helps.

The employee assistance programme offers free, confidential counselling by phone or video, available 24 hours a day. Details are on the intranet under Wellbeing.
